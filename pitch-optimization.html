Kaplan Ka4-70 Pitch Optimization

Numerical research focused on modifying and optimizing localized pitch distributions on Kaplan propeller profiles to amplify open-water operating thrust parameters.

Project Overview

Working directly under faculty guidance, this research explored numerical optimization methods for ducted marine propulsion setups. The primary objective was to modify the blade pitch angle distributions systematically to evaluate efficiency gains under heavy-load towing conditions.

Methodology & Simulation Setup

  • Geometry Reconstruction: Modified the 3D surface model parameters of a standard Kaplan Ka4-70 propeller section within Rhinoceros.
  • MRF Setup: Applied Moving Reference Frame (MRF) techniques in STAR-CCM+ to accurately define precise rotational velocity constraints.
  • Turbulence Modeling: Utilized the RANS numerical fluid formulation paired with $k-\omega$ SST closure models to resolve complex vortex structures at the blade tips.
