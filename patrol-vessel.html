Project Overview

This technical ship design project involved creating a complete layout blueprint for a medium-endurance coastal patrol vessel. The architecture balances compartment space allocations with optimal hull configurations to minimize water resistance during high-speed patrols.

Stability Verification Passes

Calculations were processed across multiple displacement loading scenarios (lightship, half-load, full-load conditions). Righting arm values ($GZ$ curves) were cross-validated against standard IMO and BKI criteria parameters to ensure complete capsizing prevention safety margins.
