Analysis of Kaplan Ka4-70 Propeller Guard Using CFD Method

A thorough hydrodynamic investigation examining fluid interactions, drag profiles, and thrust recovery systems using a pre-swirl rotator implementation on propeller guards.

Abstract & Objective

While structural propeller guards provide essential physical protection for autonomous vehicles and divers, they introduce additional skin friction and drag resistance. This thesis analyzes the application of localized pre-swirl rotator fins inside the guard structure to realign oncoming wake profiles, improving overall propulsive efficiency.

Numerical Configuration

Advanced cell-refinement controls were designated inside the narrow clearance gap between the blade tips and the internal shroud walls. Boundary layer parameters were meticulously captured by calculating local target Y+ criteria, utilizing standard k-omega SST modeling to predict flow separation points accurately.
