Project Overview

Developed to support deployment tasks for an autonomous surface vessel (ASV), this project integrated twin-hull structural engineering with localized electrical and steering constraints. Hydrostatic parameters were evaluated systematically to maintain stable equilibrium during abrupt directional maneuvers.

Manufacturing Tolerances

Designed parts considering plastic shrinkage behaviors during rapid prototyping runs. The final mechanical housing layout utilized high-infill PETG configurations to secure water-resistant barriers, ensuring robust structural integrity during prolonged water surface exposure trials.
